Why no dashboard

A graph requires someone to read it. The controller already knows the answer, so it states the action and the reason in one paragraph a farmer can act on with a phone in one hand.

Water balancedemand vs applied

01 — the methodDemand measured against supply measured

A timer waters the same in rain and in drought, because a clock cannot tell the difference. The demand side here is FAO-56 Penman-Monteith — a standard physical formulation taking temperature, humidity, wind velocity and solar radiation, returning how much water the crop and the sky will remove today. The supply side is measured directly: capacitance probes sample dielectric permittivity at 15, 30 and 60 cm, in the root horizon rather than at the surface where it does not matter — either a multi-depth node, or three single-depth probes placed at those depths, which the engine then weighs 25/50/25 into one root-zone figure.

The difference is millimetres, and millimetres drive a drip sequence. Zones are independent, so a field can hold in one corner and cycle in another.
