# ClauseLens — Commercial Strategy & Go-To-Market

**Version:** 1.0  
**Target Market:** DACH Region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) B2B Legal-Tech

---

## 1. Commercial Positioning

ClauseLens occupies a unique niche in the legal-tech landscape: **The Local, Zero-Dependency, Deterministic Risk Guard for SMBs and Commercial Teams.**

Unlike enterprise CLM (Contract Lifecycle Management) suites (Ironclad, Agiloft) that cost €30,000+/year and require 6-month rollouts, and unlike probabilistic AI chat wrappers that hallucinate or transmit client data across the Atlantic, ClauseLens delivers **instant, private, predictable risk auditing in seconds**.

### Target Buyer Personas
1. **The Pragmatic SMB Managing Director (Geschäftsführer/in):** Signs 10–50 supplier/customer contracts a year. Cannot afford €400 attorney reviews for every standard NDA or SaaS terms, but cannot risk being trapped in a 24-month auto-renewal or broad liability waiver.
2. **The Tech Agency / Freelance Consultant:** Frequently receives aggressive client master services agreements (MSAs) featuring unlimited liability, non-compete traps, and total IP rights surrender.
3. **The Corporate Procurement Buyer (Einkauf):** Audits hundreds of incoming supplier terms and conditions; needs a fast batch triage tool to identify contract red flags before negotiation.
4. **Legal Tech Integrators & Law Firms:** Embeds ClauseLens via REST API or CLI into client portals for automated pre-screening before lawyer review.

## 3. Go-To-Market Channels

1. **Product-Led Growth (PLG) via Local CLI:** Developers, IT leads, and legal ops install ClauseLens via npm or run the free version locally. They immediately experience the speed and clarity, driving upgrade conversion to Professional/Enterprise for batch and HTML exports.
2. **Channel Partnerships with Freelance Platforms & Co-Working Hubs:** Bundling ClauseLens Professional with freelancer networks (e.g., Freelancermap, Malt, Uplink) as a contract negotiation safety net.
3. **Content Marketing on German Legal Traps:** Educational whitepapers and breakdowns of notorious BGB § 307 BGH rulings (e.g. invalid 2-year auto-renewals in business AGBs) driving inbound organic traffic to the landing page.
