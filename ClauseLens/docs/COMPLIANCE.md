## 1. Data Privacy & GDPR / DSGVO Posture

ClauseLens was engineered with strict **Privacy-by-Design** and **Zero-Knowledge Architecture** principles in accordance with Art. 25 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO).

### 1.1 Zero Network Transmission (Air-Gapped Guarantee)
- **Local Execution Only:** ClauseLens executes 100% locally on the host system or inside the customer's private Docker/Kubernetes cluster.
- **No Third-Party APIs:** No contract text, snippets, metadata, or IP addresses are ever transmitted to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any remote telemetry server.
- **No Data Processing Agreement (AVV / DPA) Required:** Because ClauseLens does not process personal or proprietary data on external infrastructure, no Art. 28 DSGVO processor agreement is needed for on-premise usage.

### 1.2 Zero Disk Retention for Volatile Memory Audits
When contracts are processed via CLI or the local REST API, document text is buffered in volatile RAM during analysis and discarded immediately upon completion. Temporary files are never persisted unless explicitly requested by the operator via `--json`, `--report`, or `--html` flags.

## 2. Regulatory Compliance: Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetz (RDG)

### 2.1 Scope of Operation under German Law
Under § 2 Abs. 1 of the German Legal Services Act (*Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetz* - RDG), legal services are defined as activities in specific external legal matters that require substantive legal assessment of an individual case.

ClauseLens operates strictly as an **algorithmic document analysis and risk-tagging utility**:
- Findings reflect deterministic pattern recognition against statutory reference baselines (§§ 305–310 BGB).
- Redline counter-proposals are structural drafting templates intended for commercial negotiation.
- **Explicit Disclaimer:** ClauseLens does **not** provide legal advice, does not establish an attorney-client relationship (*Mandatsverhältnis*), and cannot replace individualized counsel by a licensed attorney (*Rechtsanwältin / Rechtsanwalt*).

### 2.2 Standard Regulatory Disclaimer in Reports
Every report generated by ClauseLens (Markdown, HTML, JSON) embeds the following mandatory legal disclaimer:

> *„Hinweis: Dieser Prüfbericht wurde automatisiert erstellt und dient der kaufmännischen Vorprüfung von Vertragsrisiken. Er stellt keine Rechtsberatung im Sinne des Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetzes (RDG) dar. Für rechtlich bindende Gutachten und Einzelfallprüfungen wenden Sie sich bitte an eine zugelassene Rechtsanwältin oder einen Rechtsanwalt.“*

## 3. Security Architecture & Dependency Hygiene

### 3.1 Zero Third-Party NPM Dependencies
ClauseLens relies exclusively on native Node.js standard modules (`node:fs`, `node:path`, `node:http`, `node:crypto`, `node:zlib`). This eliminates:
- Supply-chain injection attacks (such as malicious packages in npm).
- Transitive dependency vulnerabilities.
- Version mismatch headaches and npm audit alert fatigue.

### 3.2 Audit Logging & Deterministic Verifiability
For enterprise compliance audits (ISO 27001, SOC 2), ClauseLens outputs strict deterministic JSON results conforming to `schema/findings.schema.json`. Given the exact same input file and policy profile, ClauseLens guarantees identical finding identifiers, severity ratings, and risk scores.
