# AquaMOF Harvester

**Domain:** Materials / Water — as experimental as QEL, Nobel-level chemistry, literally separate
**One-liner:** MOF-303 aluminium panel that pulls 1L water/kg/day from desert air at 20% RH, releases with low-grade solar heat — no power.

**How it works:** MOF-303 powder (Al-3,5-pyrazoledicarboxylate) press-coated on heat exchanger → night: adsorbs vapor at 30°C/20%RH → day: sealed box heats to 85°C via solar → desorbs → condenser → potable water. Cycle 1x/day, passive. Evolutionary: Yaghi 2023 Science, not yet panelized as product.
**Buyer:** Off-grid villages, NGOs, military forward bases, vineyards — panel 299 EUR, stack 10 = family/day.
**Why easy to sell despite experimental:** Water = survival; grant + humanitarian procurement, not consumer SaaS. Demonstrator on balcony in 3 months.
**Tech:** Materials + thermodynamics + simple controller, no crypto/mesh/OS.
**Standalone:** Pure water harvester.
