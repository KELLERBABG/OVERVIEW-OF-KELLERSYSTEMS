Zero-Battery Infrastructure Computing

Burying batteries in concrete guarantees failure. Smart-Matter uses mechanical strain and piezoelectric transducing for perpetual operation.

Piezoelectric Harvesting

Converts micro-deflections from passing vehicular traffic into regulated electrical energy, powering ultra-low-power microcontrollers and radios.

Through-Material RF Mesh

Optimized low-frequency magnetic induction and sub-GHz radio propagation penetrate cured concrete and moist aggregate without external antennas.

Dead-Drop Cryptographic Log

Vehicles traveling overhead seamlessly sync tamper-evident structural health logs via near-field communication, eliminating manual inspection crews.
