01 — the invariantThe window that must stay empty

After the ventricle depolarises it repolarises, and during that recovery the tissue is vulnerable: a pacing spike delivered into the T wave can induce ventricular fibrillation. The interval is the ventricular refractory period, and the safety property is simple to state and lethal to get wrong — no spike may fall inside it.

PacePulse does not implement that as a software timer that interrupts might delay. It is a hardware-gated formal invariant, proved over every transition of the DDD and VVI pacing state machines against an electrophysiology specification, so the guarantee holds regardless of what the software is doing.

02 — comparedAgainst conventional medical firmware

Medical device software is usually argued safe by testing: hardware in the loop, coverage metrics, clinical evidence. That establishes confidence. It does not establish a bound, and a buffer overflow in C firmware is not a statistical event.
