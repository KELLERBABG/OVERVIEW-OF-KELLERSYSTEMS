01 — the translationWhat a claim becomes

A patent claim is written to be argued over, not to be computed. FluidIP parses the independent claim into first-order logic predicates — in the reference case, eighteen of them from Claim 1 — and from there the question “is this already covered?” becomes a satisfiability query rather than an examiner's judgement.

[0001] A method comprising receiving a first signal, wherein the first signal is transformed according to a predetermined relation…
[0002] Parsed to: ∃x ∀y ( S(x) ∧ R(x,y) → T(y) ) — 18 predicates, 4 free variables.
[0003] Entailment against prior-art clusters: C1 ¬(PA ∨ PB) → UNSAT, no counterexample returned.

[SMT-SOLVER]Checking entailment: C_1 ¬(P_A ∨ P_B) → UNSAT, counterexample none
