# MuonScope™ Commercial Pitch & Stakeholder Briefing

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Opportunity

**The Infrastructure Crisis:** Over \$2.5 Trillion in aging global concrete bridges, dams, tunnels, and nuclear facilities suffer from internal degradation (scour, honeycombing, tendon voids, rebar corrosion) that cannot be detected by surface inspection or conventional NDT methods.

```
Why Conventional Non-Destructive Testing Fails:
  ├── Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR): Blocked by dense rebar cages & wet concrete (>50 cm blind)
  ├── Ultrasonic Pulse Echo (UPE): Severe attenuation and scattering at aggregate interfaces (>1.5 m blind)
  └── Industrial X-Ray / Gamma: Severe radiation hazard, heavy exclusion zones, legally restricted in public
```

**The MuonScope Solution:** The world's first **ultra-portable, passive cosmic-ray muon tomography system** delivering deep 3D structural X-ray equivalent imaging with **Zero Ionizing Radiation**, sub-millimeter precision, and direct IFC 4.3 BIM export.
