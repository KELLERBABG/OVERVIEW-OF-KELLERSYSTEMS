Multilateral Ring Settlement Mathematics

In traditional cross-border settlement, chained obligations between institutions tie up billions in dormant nostro/vostro balances. DSCM treats debt obligations as directed edges in a dynamic liquidity graph.

Tarjan Cycle Elimination

When Institution A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A, no actual liquidity needs to move across borders. DSCM discovers circular debt paths in $O(V + E)$ linear time:

Δ = min_{(u, v) ∈ Ring} (Obligation_{u→v}) for (u, v) in Ring: Obligation'_{u→v} = Obligation_{u→v} - Δ // Atomic state transition eliminates debt ring to zero

This collapses circular exposure atomically within single consensus blocks, liberating institutional capital for productive underwriting.

Zero-Knowledge Capital Solvency

Sovereign banks and credit unions cannot reveal their granular asset sheets or client transaction logs to competitors.

DSCM uses cryptographic range proofs over Pedersen commitments. An institution proves mathematically that its Tier-1 Capital Ratio exceeds regulatory minimums without revealing total balance volume:

C_A = g^{Assets} h^{r_A}, C_L = g^{Liabilities} h^{r_L} Prove: (Assets - Liabilities) ≥ RequiredReserve // Verified in zero-knowledge by all mesh nodes
