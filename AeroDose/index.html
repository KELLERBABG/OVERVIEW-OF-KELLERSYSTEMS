01 — the physicsConcentration is not dose

A weather station reports how dirty the air is. It cannot tell you what a person inhaled, because the same air can deliver three to three to four times the particulate to someone clearing fireline as to someone standing nearby — the difference is how hard they are breathing. A wildland firefighter under load moves 40–60 L/min through their lungs; an observer at rest moves 12–16.

So AeroDose integrates both: the measured concentration, the worker's modelled ventilation, and the fraction of each particle size that actually deposits in alveolar tissue.

Dose_total = ∫0T C_PM2.5(t) · V̇E(Workload(t)) · DFalv(dp) dt
DF_alv = fractional deposition efficiency in alveolar sac tissue, per ICRP 66

The dashed line in the chart above is what the same air would have delivered to a resting observer. The gap between the two lines is the part of the hazard that concentration monitoring misses entirely.
