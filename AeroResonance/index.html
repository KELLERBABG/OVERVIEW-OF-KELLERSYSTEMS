01 — the mechanismLock-in, not rotation

A wind turbine needs blades, bearings and about 3 m/s before it produces anything. AeroResonance uses Von Kármán vortex shedding: air moving past a bluff body sheds alternating low-pressure vortices, and the cavity behind it is tuned so its Helmholtz mode sits where that shedding lands.

When the two frequencies coincide the oscillation amplitude multiplies — the shaded band above — and that amplified pressure differential drives a PVDF cantilever array. Nothing rotates, so nothing wears.

02 — the second sourceThe sky is already oscillating

The Earth–ionosphere cavity resonates at 7.83 Hz and its harmonics, driven continuously by global lightning. AeroResonance couples a high-permeability core coil to that fundamental, so a node keeps trickle-charging in still air, indoors, inside a sealed structure.

let flux  = coil.integrate_flux(schumann_band);
let v_ind = -1.0 * turns * (flux.delta() / dt);
rectifier.stage_charge(v_ind);

Two sources, one rectifier: acoustic pressure when there is weather, magnetic induction when there is not.
