01 — what it doesCapture, weigh, sign

A filter that nobody measures is a filter nobody can claim. The section above shows the three parts that matter: a 20 µm micro-mesh fine enough to catch fibre rather than lint, an acoustic back-wash that keeps the cake from choking an 80 kg machine mid-cycle, and a sealed sludge chamber on load cells so the trapped mass is a measurement rather than an estimate.

[CREDIT-MINT]Ed25519 microfiber removal certificate signed for textile brand

02 — why the ledger mattersA removal claim has to survive an auditor

Environmental claims collapse when the evidence is a spreadsheet the claimant controls. Here the mass reading is taken inside a microcontroller enclave, differential pressure and load-cell series are signed at source, and the resulting removal credit carries a signature a textile brand can hand to a regulator instead of a summary.
